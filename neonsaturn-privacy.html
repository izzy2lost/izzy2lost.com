Privacy Policy for NEON SATURN

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Introduction

NEON SATURN ("we," "our," or "us") is an Android application for Sega Saturn emulation powered by the Ymir emulation core (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the Service accesses, stores, and shares.

Information We Do Not Collect

NEON SATURN is designed to run locally on your device. We do not operate an account or analytics service, and we do not intentionally collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or other personal identification information
  • Contact lists or precise location data
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Usage analytics, telemetry, or device identifiers for profiling
  • Your disc images, BIOS files, save data, or emulator state

Information Stored or Accessed Locally

To provide emulator functionality, NEON SATURN may store or access the following on your device:

  • App preferences, emulator settings, themes, and touch control layouts
  • Game library metadata, including game names and file locations
  • Game folders and files you explicitly choose through Android file-selection flows
  • A Saturn BIOS (IPL) ROM, and optionally a CD block ROM, that you explicitly import
  • Copies of games staged into the app's own storage so they can be launched
  • Save states, internal backup RAM saves, and persistent Saturn clock and BIOS settings
  • Downloaded cover art

Permissions and File Access

NEON SATURN uses Android's Storage Access Framework so you can import a BIOS ROM and select the folder holding your disc images. Access is governed by the files and folders you select; the app does not request broad storage permissions. Selected games are copied into the app's own storage before launch so the emulator can open them as ordinary files. NEON SATURN does not upload your selected game or BIOS files.

Network Use and Third-Party Services

Core emulation works entirely offline. NEON SATURN uses internet access only to download game cover artwork from a repository hosted on GitHub, either on demand while browsing your library or in bulk when you choose to download all covers. The requested artwork path can identify a game title or region. GitHub may also receive standard connection information such as your IP address and user agent. Review GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

The app does not send disc images, BIOS files, save data, or emulator state to the cover-art service.

Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell or share your personal data. NEON SATURN does not operate a remote account or cloud-save backend. Standard network information is processed by GitHub only when it serves optional cover-art files, as described above.

Data Retention and Deletion

App data, staged games, save states, and cached artwork are stored locally on your device. You can remove them by clearing NEON SATURN's storage or uninstalling the app through Android system settings. Cover art can also be cleared from within the app's settings. Files you placed outside the app's storage, such as your original disc images, may need to be deleted separately using your device's file manager.

Device Backups

Depending on your Android device and backup settings, Android may include eligible NEON SATURN app data in a system backup managed by your device or backup provider. You can manage or disable device backups in Android settings. NEON SATURN does not operate its own backup or cloud-sync service.

Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security

Because NEON SATURN primarily stores data locally, its security depends on your device and the storage locations you select. Keep your device updated, use its available security features, and only install game files and BIOS ROMs from sources you trust.

No Games Included / Legal Notice

NEON SATURN does not include games, disc images, BIOS files, or commercial artwork. Only use content you are legally permitted to use. NEON SATURN is not affiliated with or endorsed by Sega.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page and reflected in the "Last updated" date.
